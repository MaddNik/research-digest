This short paper resolves the relationship between two recently introduced lattice assumptions: Succinct Learning with Errors, introduced by Wee at CRYPTO 2024, and Decomposed Learning with Errors, introduced by Abram, Malavolta, and Roy at CRYPTO 2025. Abram, Malavolta, and Roy had already shown that Succinct LWE implies Decomposed LWE, and this paper establishes the converse implication, that Decomposed LWE implies Succinct LWE. Together the two results show the assumptions are equivalent under appropriate parameter settings, clarifying the foundational landscape of lattice assumptions that underpin succinct lattice-based cryptographic constructions.
