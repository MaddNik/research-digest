The authors present a framework that merges programmable integrated photonics with high-speed optical characterization to control the spectral and temporal properties of light over a broad bandwidth, replacing bulky and inflexible bulk-optic pulse shapers. Using machine learning to tailor nonlinear pulse propagation, they experimentally demonstrate scalable, on-demand shaping of broadband ultrafast pulse patterns. The system achieves reconfigurable power control across a 400 nanometer bandwidth with picosecond resolution. The authors note applications spanning biomedical imaging, lidar, ultrafast spectroscopy, quantum information processing, and optical computing, where flexible control over multiphoton excitations is needed.
