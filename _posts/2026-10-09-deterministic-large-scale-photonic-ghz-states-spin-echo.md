The authors demonstrate a semiconductor quantum dot based device that deterministically generates, at gigahertz rates, indistinguishable single photons entangled in a robust Greenberger-Horne-Zeilinger (GHZ) state spanning more than a dozen photons. The device exploits a novel natural spin-echo concept arising from the opposite precession directions of the negative and positive carriers' spins in the excited and ground levels of the periodically excited dot. The excitation rate is tuned so that the phase accumulated by both spin precessions cancels out, preserving coherence over the extended photon train. The authors note that this record-length GHZ-state source can plausibly be improved further to provide higher-rate, brighter, and more robust deterministic entanglement generation, which would reduce the resource overhead needed for photonic quantum technologies.
