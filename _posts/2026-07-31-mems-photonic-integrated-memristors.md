The authors introduce an electrically programmable optical storage device that uses integrated micro-electromechanical components fabricated with standard semiconductor processes, avoiding the need to integrate additional non-standard materials into the photonic platform. The device achieves 5-bit phase storage levels with programming speeds of 50 kilobits per second and demonstrates endurance beyond one billion program cycles. Non-volatile storage extends from over one hour up to several days when combined with refresh circuitry, and the team validated the approach with a prototype optical random-access memory system. This represents progress toward practical, foundry-compatible non-volatile optical memory elements for reconfigurable photonic circuits.
