---

Integrated photon-pair sources underpin chip-based quantum computing, communication, and metrology, but most demonstrations to date operate at conventional telecom wavelengths, leaving the 2 micrometer band comparatively unexplored despite its advantages for free-space quantum communication, low-loss transmission in emerging fiber networks, and compatibility with silicon photonic platforms. The authors demonstrate spontaneous parametric down-conversion in straight GaAs- and AlGaAs-on-insulator waveguides, a platform chosen for its strong second-order nonlinearity and geometry-tunable dispersion. Measurements of the joint spectral intensity and the heralded second-order correlation function reveal broadband emission around 2 micrometers with strong spectral anti-correlations between the generated photon pairs. The authors state this is the first direct joint-spectral characterization of an integrated spontaneous parametric down-conversion source in this wavelength regime.
