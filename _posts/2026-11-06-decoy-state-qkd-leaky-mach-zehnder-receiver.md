---

This work proves the security of phase-encoded decoy-state BB84 quantum key distribution for a Mach-Zehnder receiver that has imbalanced beam splitters and two threshold detectors leaking fine-grained information about detection patterns. The authors derive a lower bound on the legitimate receiver's single-photon contributions and combine it with the decoy-state method and a recent phase-error framework built for leaky receivers. The correction to the phase-error rate caused by this leakage is independent of channel loss, giving a loss-tolerant treatment of receiver imperfections even when the beam splitters are imbalanced. The result provides a practical route toward loss-tolerant security proofs for real quantum key distribution receivers that are not perfectly characterized.
