SM3 is the 256-bit cryptographic hash function specified in the Chinese national standard GB/T 32905-2016 and the international standard ISO/IEC 10118-3:2018. This paper presents practical collisions for 22-step and 23-step SM3 using the standard initial value, extending the previous best result of a 20-step collision by two and three additional steps respectively. The time complexities of the new constructions are approximately 2 to the 52.4 power and 2 to the 54.5 power evaluations of the reduced-step SM3 compression function. The authors also present a semi-free-start collision for 24-step SM3, pushing the best known practical attacks on this widely deployed hash function further than previously demonstrated.
