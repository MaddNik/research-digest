description: "A programmable photonic circuit reconstructs the full optical coherence matrix of partially coherent light using only intensity measurements, without interferometric phase retrieval."
---

The paper targets a limitation in characterizing spatial optical coherence, where traditional methods rely on phase-sensitive interferometry that is highly sensitive to environmental noise and does not scale well for integrated photonic implementations. The authors introduce an architecture-agnostic framework, based on the Schur-Horn theorem, that diagonalizes an incident coherence matrix on programmable photonic circuits using only output intensity measurements, avoiding complex phase retrieval entirely. They experimentally validate the black-box protocol on a low-depth, non-universal photonic integrated circuit, successfully reconstructing the eigenvalues of mixed states generated from up to four mutually incoherent sources. The work also demonstrates active, in-situ control of statistical light properties through non-unitary amplitude modulation, which the authors report reveals a trade-off between enhancing interference visibility and optical loss. The authors describe this as a scalable pathway toward compact, on-chip spatial coherence analyzers.
