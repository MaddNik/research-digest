---

Optical computing promises speed and energy efficiency gains for AI workloads, but implementing efficient nonlinear activation functions purely with light has remained difficult. The authors present an optical nonlinear neural processing unit, or ONNPU, that implements all-optical nonlinear activation through a self-rectification mechanism, and whose architecture mirrors the structure of digital neural networks so it can plug into existing deep learning pipelines. They benchmark the ONNPU on nine varied tasks covering decision, regression, and generation, reporting 98.07 percent accuracy on MNIST and 93.54 percent on Fashion-MNIST. When integrated into a 201 million parameter Vision Transformer it reaches 82.4 percent top-1 accuracy on full 1,000 category ImageNet classification, and when integrated into a 117 million parameter decoder-only Transformer it enables short-form story generation that the authors report outperforms GPT-2. The results point toward optical hardware that can handle more complex and diverse deep learning tasks than earlier photonic nonlinear activation demonstrations.
