description: "A telecom-wavelength KTP waveguide source produces extremely bright, near-single-mode squeezed light for hybrid continuous-variable and discrete-variable quantum photonics."
---

The paper targets hybrid quantum photonics, which combines continuous-variable squeezed states with discrete-variable photon-counting measurements, a combination that requires squeezed-vacuum light occupying a single well-defined optical mode. The authors present a single-pass, periodically poled, Type-II potassium titanyl phosphate waveguide engineered to meet this requirement while remaining compatible with existing single-photon detectors and fiber networks. The reported source produces a single-mode squeezed-vacuum state with a measured effective mode number of 1.24, generates up to 40,000 photons per pulse, and operates at a central wavelength of 1546 nanometers with picosecond pulse duration, which the authors note enables intrinsic photon-number resolution when paired with superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors. They also describe a theoretical framework for photon-counting-based characterization of the source under high-gain, lossy operating conditions. The work is presented as addressing brightness, mode purity, scalability, and detector compatibility simultaneously, which the authors state has been difficult to achieve together in one platform.
