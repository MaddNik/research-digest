---

McEliece-type systems sometimes protect their public Generalized Reed-Solomon code structure using masking matrices with elevated row and column weight to resist structural attacks. Prior work analyzed weights between 1 and 1 plus the code rate, but the weight-2 case in the high-rate regime had not been examined. The authors introduce cube-code-based distinguishing techniques that detect the underlying Generalized Reed-Solomon structure in these high-rate, weight-2-masked public codes, then give a general methodology for turning such a distinguisher into a full key-recovery attack. They demonstrate the attack succeeds against schemes using weight-2 masking in the high-rate setting.
