byline: "Rohan Radhakrishnan et al. (arXiv:2608.24495, Aug 25 2026)"
description: "AlGaAs nanowires are shown to serve as a single, unified growth platform capable of hosting GaAs, InGaAs, and InAs quantum dots with tunable emission across the visible-to-near-infrared range."
---

Optical quantum dots are central to photonic quantum technologies, but fabrication approaches are typically tailored to a single spectral range, and a unified platform combining one growth method with a designable host architecture has been missing. The authors introduce AlGaAs nanowires as a universal host, building on their prior demonstration of high-quality GaAs quantum dots to realize InGaAs quantum dots with tunable emission by varying growth duration between 2 and 5 seconds, achieving emission at 780 and 920 nanometers. They further show the platform's versatility for multi-quantum-dot devices by co-integrating two InGaAs quantum dots, as well as GaAs and InGaAs quantum dots, within a single nanowire. Finally, they take a first step toward pure InAs quantum dots by growing a pristine InAs segment on AlGaAs nanowires, demonstrating material compatibility for future wavelength extension.
