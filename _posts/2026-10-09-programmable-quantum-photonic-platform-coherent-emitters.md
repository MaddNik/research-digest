The authors demonstrate a programmable quantum photonic platform that integrates semiconductor quantum dot single-photon emitters with reconfigurable photonic circuitry on a single chip operated at cryogenic temperatures. The device allows simultaneous, individual electrical and optical addressing of active components while preserving coherent, non-classical light emission from the embedded emitters. They show several quantum-photonic functionalities on this platform, including pure single-photon generation, two-photon interference, and resonant transmission and reflection measurements of the integrated emitters. This system-level integration marks a step from isolated quantum-optical devices toward programmable quantum-photonic hardware that can combine coherent emitters with large-scale photonic circuit functionality.
