---

The authors present an integrated photonics platform on lithium tantalate-on-insulator where the ferroelectric-domain configuration used for phase tuning remains in place after the write field is removed, giving non-volatile, zero static power operation. The platform achieves a low waveguide propagation loss of about 0.05 to 0.06 decibels per centimeter and demonstrates multilevel non-volatile phase tuning stable through 1 million write cycles. Using weighted segmented electrodes, they reach 137 distinct phase positions with about 0.007 pi resolution. They combine this non-volatile phase control with a high-speed electro-optic modulator operating above 110 gigahertz to achieve zero-static-power bias control, and they build an image-edge-detection chip reaching 3.48 tera-operations per second per watt. This suggests a route toward low-power, reconfigurable photonic circuits that hold their state without continuous power draw.
