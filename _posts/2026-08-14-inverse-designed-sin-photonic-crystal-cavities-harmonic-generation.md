The authors used inverse-design methods to engineer a two-dimensional silicon nitride photonic crystal cavity, reporting a quality factor of approximately 80,000, which they describe as the highest value reported for a near-stoichiometric silicon nitride 2D photonic crystal cavity. The strong optical confinement, expressed as a high quality-factor-to-mode-volume ratio, was validated experimentally by observing both second-harmonic and third-harmonic generation from the same cavity structure. Producing both nonlinear responses from a single inverse-designed cavity illustrates the strength of the achieved confinement. The authors present the result as a promising building block for nonlinear photonics and for future heterogeneous integrated photonic devices.
