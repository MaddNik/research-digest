The authors demonstrate a photonic processor based on a six-channel universal chip fabricated by femtosecond laser writing with an interlaced architecture of multiport beam splitters. They extend a calibration procedure developed for a single building block to the complete six-channel interferometer and reconstruct interferometer models over a broad spectral range. Testing against 100 Haar-random unitary matrices yields mean amplitude fidelities of 95.9 percent, 98.0 percent, and 96.0 percent at 910, 945, and 980 nanometers respectively, without on-device optimization. The chip also performs spectral demultiplexing and multiplexing at these wavelengths with routing fidelities from 90.1 to 96.5 percent, confirming its use for practical wavelength-routing tasks.
