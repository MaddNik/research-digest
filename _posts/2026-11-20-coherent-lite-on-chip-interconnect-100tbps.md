The authors present a coherent-lite multidimensional on-chip interconnect aimed at the bandwidth and energy demands of AI compute scaling. The design co-transmits a local oscillator and parallel data channels within a single silicon waveguide, using polarization-division routing to keep the local oscillator clean and support high-order modulation without power-hungry digital carrier recovery. A key component is an inverse-designed dual-polarization mode multiplexer that achieves over 37 decibels of inter-polarization isolation across the C band in an 8 by 6 square micrometer footprint, enabling millimeter-scale on-chip multimode routing. The system delivers a single-wavelength line rate of 2 terabits per second and scales to an aggregate line rate of 103.96 terabits per second across four modes and 100 wavelengths using modulation formats up to 64-ary quadrature amplitude modulation. The results extend on-chip coherent interconnects into the hundred-terabit-per-second regime for dense optical connectivity in future computing systems.
