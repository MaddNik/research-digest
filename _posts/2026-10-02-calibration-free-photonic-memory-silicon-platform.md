The authors propose a calibration-free photonic memory bitcell built from nonresonant variable optical attenuators in the GlobalFoundries 45SPCLO silicon photonics platform. Because the cell maintains its complementary electrical states through electro-optical feedback rather than cavity-resonance alignment, it avoids the calibration overhead that resonance-based photonic memories typically require. Wordline-controlled attenuators enable selective writing through differential optical bitlines, while optical readout shares the same holding input and output splitters, so no additional read attenuators or separate laser source are needed. Circuit simulations show writing with 10 picosecond optical pulses at 2 milliwatts peak power, data retention as long as optical and electrical power are supplied, and an incremental write energy of 0.562 picojoules including the laser's contribution at 20 percent wall-plug efficiency. The bitcell layout occupies about 147.92 by 219.93 square micrometers.
