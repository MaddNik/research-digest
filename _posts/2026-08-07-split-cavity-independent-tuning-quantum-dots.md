byline: "Lan Hoang Mai et al. (arXiv:2608.02388, Aug 3 2026)"
description: "A GaAs photonic crystal cavity split into two electrically isolated halves allows independent electric-field tuning of two InAs quantum dots while keeping a quality factor above 20,000."
---

Bringing more than one solid-state qubit into resonance with a shared cavity mode normally requires independent tuning, which is difficult in a single continuous photonic crystal cavity. The authors design, fabricate, and characterize a GaAs-based photonic crystal split cavity made of two electrically distinct portions, preserving the ability to apply separate electric fields that could independently tune two InAs quantum dots into resonance with the same cavity mode. Despite the physical cut needed to electrically isolate the two halves, the device achieves an average quality factor of at least 20,000, which is sufficient to reach the strong coupling regime. The authors find that this quality factor is limited mainly by the precision and accuracy of the electron-beam lithography tool rather than by sidewall scattering losses. They describe this as a realistic experimental path toward devices that use cavity-mediated strong coupling between two InAs quantum dots to generate multi-dimensional photon graph states.
