The paper proposes an end-to-end design methodology for multimode interferometer based photonic devices that merges conventional parameter optimization with topology optimization, while staying within foundry manufacturing constraints. The seeded approach starts topology optimization from an existing parameterized design rather than from scratch, which the authors say opens up design possibilities beyond what parameterized techniques alone can reach. They test the method on three device types, including a 1x2 power splitter and a polarization splitter, and report that seeded topology optimization reduces the measured insertion loss of the 1x2 splitter from 0.20 to 0.14 decibels, along with improved performance for a modal multiplexer across a range of wavelengths. The authors frame the work as a step toward incorporating topology-optimized components directly into commercial photonic foundry design kits with improved fabrication robustness.
