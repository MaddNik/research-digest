---

The paper presents an integrated silicon photonic system aimed at AI infrastructure communication needs, combining transceiver functions with spatial and polarization processing on a single chip. The system achieves a communication capacity exceeding 1.8 terabits per second per wavelength channel by handling multiple data dimensions simultaneously on-chip, which the authors say reduces power consumption and latency relative to conventional digital signal processing approaches. They report successful full-duplex communication testing over 300 meters of fiber. The work targets next-generation data center optical interconnects that need to scale bandwidth density for AI workloads.
