---

Quantum key distribution network nodes are generally assumed to be secure at the physical layer, but the authors point out that actual QKD equipment may still be vulnerable to side-channel attacks such as electromagnetic leakage. They measured electromagnetic emissions from a prototype QKD device implementing a time-bin decoy-state BB84 protocol with Y and Z bases, recording electromagnetic spectra and waveforms at distances of 0 and 3 meters from both the transmitter and receiver inside an anechoic chamber. The results identify the transmitter as the primary source of electromagnetic leakage, and the measurements show distinct differences between the emissions generated for different quantum states at the third harmonic of the device's 1241.6 megahertz clock. The paper is published in IEEE Access.
