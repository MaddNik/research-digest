Coupling light efficiently between photonic integrated circuits and optical fibers remains a practical challenge for silicon-lithium niobate (Si-LN) photonics. The authors propose a fully-etched Si-LN grating coupler design in which subwavelength structures engineer the coupling strength to improve directionality, combined with adjoint-based inverse design for efficient fiber coupling. Accounting for the birefringence of lithium niobate, the couplers are optimized and fabricated for the TE mode along both the crystallographic Z and Y directions using only a single-step silicon etch. The measured coupling efficiencies are -3.3 decibels and -3.9 decibels along the Z and Y directions respectively, with 3-decibel bandwidths broader than 54 nanometers and back reflections suppressed to below -15 decibels over 50 nanometers in both directions. The single-etch fabrication process offers a practical route to fast device prototyping in Si-LN photonic integrated circuits.
