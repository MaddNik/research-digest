---

The team built a broadband photonic integrated circuit intended to control individual trapped ions for quantum information processing. The device combines a planar waveguide lens with a micromirror fabricated using two-photon polymerization at wafer scale, producing what the authors describe as a monolithic photonic architecture. It demonstrates the ability to individually address three ions across wavelengths spanning 405 to 880 nanometers with minimal optical crosstalk between channels. The authors validated the approach experimentally with both calcium and barium trapped ions, confirming individual repumping capability for each ion. This targets a key scaling bottleneck for trapped-ion quantum computers, which need compact, broadband optical addressing hardware integrated at the chip level.
